Questions

What do I get for $150?

A fifteen-minute session and five edited photos, delivered in a private online gallery within two weeks — in good time for cards and printing.

If you'd rather have everything from your session than my five picks, you can add the extended gallery for $50.

Is fifteen minutes actually enough?

For this, yes. The set is already built and lit, so none of your time goes on setting up — we start the moment you walk in.

Fifteen minutes is also about the honest limit of a toddler's patience.

When do we get the photos?

Within two weeks of the day, in a private online gallery. You download them at full resolution and print them wherever you like.

What should we wear?

Christmas clothes are very welcome — that's half the fun of it. If that's not your thing, wear whatever everyone is comfortable in instead.

Neutrals sit beautifully against the set, and textures like knits, cord and wool read better on camera than flat block colour. Rather than everyone matching exactly, pick colours that complement each other. Unless you're all going in matching Christmas pyjamas, which is its own kind of excellent.

What if my child won't play along?

Expected, honestly. I'm a dad of two and most of my working week is spent around small children with their own plans.

We work with whatever the day gives us. The frames people end up loving are usually the in-between ones, not the one where everybody finally looked at the camera at once.

How many of us can come?

The set comfortably holds two adults and the kids. If you're a larger group, get in touch before you book and we'll work out what's possible.

Can we bring the dog?

Not at this one, sorry — it's people only.

Where do we park?

There's ample parking at the community hall, so there's no need to arrive early hunting for a spot.

What if we can't make our time?

Let me know as early as you can. If another time on the day is still free, I'll move you across to it — that's usually the easiest fix.

Everything runs on the one day, Sunday 8 November, so there's no other date to move to.

If you need to cancel more than seven days before the session, you'll be refunded half the fee. Inside seven days, the session isn't refundable.

That's everything. Limited spots, one day only.
