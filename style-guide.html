What to wear, and why it matters

The best photos happen when everyone feels like themselves.

Being comfortable in your clothes shapes how you feel in front of the camera. Choose clothes that you feel free, comfortable and you can be yourself in.

The palette

These are the colours my editing loves: soft earth tones, olives, dusty blues and clay pinks, with a few bolder accents. Pick two or three for each person and let them sit together rather than match, with a bold accent or pattern if you love one.

What photographs well

To start, anything you feel the most you in. If you’d like a bit of guidance, below are suggestions, not hard rules.

  • Textures
  • Denim
  • Dresses you feel you in
  • Bold, complementary colours and patterns
  • Sneakers, sandals and even bare feet
  • If you have tattoos and you’re comfortable, flaunt them

Tips

Wear what you feel comfortable in. If you think something might not work or are still looking for ideas, send me a message and we can talk it through.
