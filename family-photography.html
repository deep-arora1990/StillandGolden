Family photography for the season you're in right now

The chaos, the cuddles, and the real moments in between.

Every stage of family life arrives quietly and leaves before you're ready. The toddler who insists on the same bedtime book every night. The baby who's suddenly not a baby anymore. The way your kids pile onto the couch on a Sunday morning. A family session documents whatever season you're in — not a stiff, matching-outfits version of it, but the real one.

I'm Deep, a dad of two based in South-East Melbourne. My approach is documentary-style: I give gentle direction where it helps, then step back and photograph your family being your family. The result is a gallery of warm, honest images that actually feel like your life.

At home, or out in the world

Family sessions run in whichever setting suits your crew best:

  • In-home sessions — pancakes, pillow fights, book piles and bedroom cuddles. Perfect for young kids who are happiest on their own turf, and for capturing the home your family is growing up in.
  • Outdoor sessions — a favourite park, the beach, or somewhere like George Pentland Gardens in Frankston. Golden-hour light, room to run, and plenty of energy to burn.

Either way, there's no pressure for anyone to perform. Toddlers who won't sit still, kids pulling faces, a baby who needs a feed mid-session — all of it is normal, and most of it ends up being the best photos.

What a session looks like

  • Around an hour of relaxed shooting — longer if the little ones need warm-up time.
  • Gentle prompts, not poses. Games, tickles and questions that get real laughs, not "say cheese".
  • Everyone welcome. Grandparents, new babies, the family dog — if they're part of your season, they belong in the photos.
  • A full edited gallery within 2 weeks, delivered through a private online gallery.
