Common questions

When should I book my newborn session?

Ideally while you're still pregnant — most families book in their second or third trimester to secure a spot. The session itself happens any time within the first 4 weeks after birth, and I keep the exact date flexible around your baby's actual arrival.

Does my house need to be tidy or "photo-ready"?

Not at all. I work with the natural light from your windows and usually only need two or three spots — a bed, a couch, the nursery. The rest of the house can look exactly like a house with kids in it.

What should we wear?

Soft, simple and comfortable works best — think warm neutrals and gentle tones rather than big logos or bright fluoro. Once you book, I'll send through a short guide with easy outfit ideas for the whole family.

How does the full-gallery upgrade work?

After your session you'll receive your included images, and you can then unlock the rest of the edited gallery — $75 on Glimpse, $50 on Golden. Gathered and Bloom already include the full gallery, so there's nothing extra to decide.

How long until we receive our photos?

Your edited gallery is delivered within 2 weeks of your session, through a private online gallery you can download and share with family.
