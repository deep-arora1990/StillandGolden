Christmas Mini Session Terms

Sunday 8 November 2026 · Baxter Community Hall. Please read before completing your booking.

These terms apply to Christmas mini sessions only. They replace the general Photography Session Agreement, which covers Glimpse, Golden, Gathered and Bloom bookings and does not apply to this event.

What's Included

A 15-minute session on Sunday 8 November 2026 at Baxter Community Hall, 211 Baxter-Tooradin Rd, Baxter VIC 3911, and 5 professionally edited photographs.

Images are delivered via a private online gallery within 2 weeks of the session date, as high-resolution JPEG files for personal use.

The extended gallery — every edited image from your session rather than the 5 selected — is available for an additional $50, and can be added on the day.

The Day

Sessions run back to back on a fixed timetable, from 10:30am with the last session at 4:20pm. You choose your time when you book.

Because each session is 15 minutes and the next family is waiting, a session that starts late still finishes at its scheduled time. Please arrive a few minutes before your booked start.

Payment

The session fee is $150, payable in full at the time of booking. Your time is not reserved until payment is complete.

Changes, Cancellation & Refunds

Changing your time. If another session time on the day is still available, you may move to it at no charge. Please ask as early as you can.

No alternative date. This is a single-day event. There is no other date to reschedule to, so a change of date is not possible.

Cancelling. Cancellations made more than 7 days before 8 November 2026 are refunded at 50% of the session fee. Cancellations made within 7 days of the session date are not refundable.

If the photographer cancels. In the event of illness or other unforeseen circumstances preventing the session from going ahead, you will be offered an alternative time where one is available, or a full refund.

Image Usage & Rights

Client use: Images are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. The client may print, share, and display images for personal purposes. Commercial use of any image is strictly prohibited without prior written consent from Still & Golden Photography.

Photographer use: Still & Golden Photography retains full copyright of all images. The photographer reserves the right to use images for portfolio, website, social media, and other promotional purposes. If you would prefer your images not be used publicly, please notify the photographer in writing prior to your session.

General

This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the Christmas mini session and is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.
