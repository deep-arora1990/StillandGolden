Photography Session Agreement

Please read carefully and complete the acceptance form below.

What's Included

Every session is delivered as a private online gallery within 2 weeks of the session date. The gallery stays online for 2 months, and images download as high-resolution JPEG files for personal use. The number of professionally edited photographs included depends on the session booked:

  • Glimpse — up to 45 minutes, 10 edited photographs. The full gallery may be added for $150.
  • Golden — up to 90 minutes, 20 edited photographs. The full gallery may be added for $125.
  • Gathered — 90 minutes, 30 or more edited photographs, with the full gallery included.
  • Bloom — two separate 90-minute sessions (a maternity or family session, and a newborn session), each delivered as its own gallery of 30 or more edited photographs, with the full gallery included.
  • Seasonal offers (In-home Christmas session, Sunset beach mini) — 45 minutes, with the full gallery included. A beach mini takes place at the beach chosen at booking, timed around sunset, with the start time confirmed by the photographer.

Choosing your images: Your online gallery shows every edited image from your session. For Glimpse and Golden sessions, you choose the images included in your session (10 or 20) and download those at no extra cost. The full gallery can be added through your online gallery at any time within the 2 months it is online. Gathered, Bloom and seasonal offer sessions include the full gallery, so every image can be downloaded.

Payment Terms

Payment is required at the time of booking to secure the session date. All payments are non-refundable in the event of a change of mind or cancellation within 7 days of the scheduled session date.

Paying in two parts. Where it is offered at the time of booking, you may pay half the session fee to secure your date and the remaining half seven days later, charged automatically to the same card. You will be reminded three days beforehand, and nothing is charged after the second payment. Part payment is only available when your session is more than nine days away.

If the second payment does not go through, your session is still booked. You will be emailed a link to pay with another card, and the balance remains due before the session.

The full fee must be paid before your session takes place. If the second payment has not cleared by then, the session cannot go ahead until it has.

If you cancel having paid only the first half, the same terms apply as to any other payment: more than 7 days before your session it is refunded, and within 7 days it is not. Either way the second payment is simply not taken, so cancelling never costs more than you have already paid.

If the photographer cancels or cannot deliver the session, everything you have paid is refunded in full, and any payment still to come is cancelled.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

All payments are non-refundable in the event of a change of mind or cancellation within 7 days of the scheduled session date.

Rescheduling is available at no additional charge and is subject to mutual agreement between the client and photographer. The photographer reserves the right to reschedule in the event of illness, extreme weather, or other unforeseen circumstances, with an alternative date offered at no extra cost. Rescheduling does not change when a second payment is taken.

Newborn sessions: Rescheduling is available at no charge if baby has not yet arrived by the scheduled date, or if baby, the client, or a member of the immediate household is unwell. Please notify the photographer as soon as possible so an alternative date can be arranged within the newborn window.

Image Usage & Rights

Client use: Images are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. The client may print, share, and display images for personal purposes. Commercial use of any image is strictly prohibited without prior written consent from Still & Golden Photography.

Photographer use: Still & Golden Photography retains full copyright of all images. The photographer reserves the right to use images for portfolio, website, social media, and other promotional purposes. If you would prefer your images not be used publicly, please notify the photographer in writing prior to your session.
